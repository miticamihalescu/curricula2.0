### Exercise 1 — Fill in the blanks (20 points, 2p each)

Use the verbs in brackets in Past Simple or Past Continuous:

1. While I _______________ (study) for the exam, my phone _______________ (ring).
2. Yesterday, Maria _______________ (go) to the cinema with her friends.
3. They _______________ (play) football when it _______________ (start) to rain.
4. At 8 o'clock last night, my family _______________ (watch) TV.
5. Tom _______________ (not finish) his homework because he _______________ (feel) ill.

---

### Exercise 2 — Choose the correct form (15 points, 3p each)

Circle the correct answer:

1. When I arrived, he (was sleeping / slept) on the sofa.
2. She (was cooking / cooked) dinner every day last week.
3. I (was seeing / saw) a great film yesterday evening.
4. While he (drove / was driving) to work, he (heard / was hearing) the news on the radio.
5. They (were living / lived) in Paris for three years (then they moved).

---
